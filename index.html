About

Living & Hospitality, end to end.

Realaize Capital is a real estate investment platform specialised in Living and Hospitality in Europe, with primary focus on Spain. We act as asset and development manager and as operating partner for international investors, hotel groups, family offices and institutional funds.

We cover the whole value chain — origination, due diligence, structuring, financing, development, asset management and exit — with proprietary technology that sources off-market opportunities around the clock.
